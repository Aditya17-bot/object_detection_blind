Mode

Detections

    Position

    Each box becomes a clock bearing (12 o'clock is straight ahead) and a proximity bucket. Thresholds are per class, so a "close" bottle is a far smaller box than a "close" person.

    Decision

    Walk mode only speaks about obstacles that are close or very close, and says which side is freer. Low-confidence names are spoken as "obstacle" rather than risk a wrong noun.

    Two models, one answer

    A COCO YOLOv8n plus a fine-tune for doors, which COCO lacks. Overlapping boxes from the two are merged by IoU, so one object is never announced under two names.

    A still-image demo of the Android app's pipeline; the app adds persistence and cooldown across frames, offline voice commands, sonar and OCR. Distances are rough estimates from box height. Not a safety device.
